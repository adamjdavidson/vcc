# Cheese Narrative project files

Working documents from the "Cheese Narrative" Claude Project: the second-generation story kit, the "Your Cheese Story" app specs, cheese-style histories, and buyer and story research. Copied here as-is from the project (October 2026).

These sit alongside, not inside, the original kit in the rest of this repository. Nothing here has been wired into `START_HERE_FOR_LLM.md` or `REPO-MAP.md`.

## Contents

- `your-cheese-story-spec-v2.md`, `cheese-story-app-spec.md`: specs for the "Your Cheese Story" app (v2 is current).
- `kit/`: the new kit.
  - `core-brief.md`, `flow.md`, `story-arcs.md`, `lenses.md`, `personas.md`: how the app works and the story frameworks.
  - Output pieces: `for-the-table.md`, `shelf-talker.md`, `monger-card.md`, `monger-answer-site.md`, `packaging-and-label.md`, `website-story.md`, `photo-essay.md`, `video-concept.md`, `social-posts.md`, `press-release.md`, `pr-research-and-pitches.md`.
  - `history/`: sourced histories of Vermont cheese (1985–2026) and of each style (alpine, bloomy rind, blue, cheddar, goat, gouda, pasta filata, sheep's milk, tomme, washed rind), plus `alpine-deep-history.md`.
  - `research/`: who buys artisanal cheese, and which story elements work, with citations.
- `research/selling-artisanal-food-through-story.md`: long-form research on selling artisanal food through story.

Not included: the project's copy of Paul Kindstedt's *Cheese and Culture* (a copyrighted book).
