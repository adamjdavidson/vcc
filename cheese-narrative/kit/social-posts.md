# Social Posts

**Purpose:** A set of three short posts the maker can publish this week, each built on one concrete moment from their story.

## Who reads it and how

People scrolling: regular customers, farm-store visitors, mongers who follow the farm, and the occasional writer. They give a post about two seconds. A specific moment holds them; a general claim doesn't.

## What it needs from the Creamery Card

The chosen story, and two or three specific moments, details, or facts that could each carry a post on its own: a day in the make room, the herd going out to pasture, a named cow, a batch that failed, a customer's reaction, a neighbor's barn.

## The set

1. **Instagram caption** (40 to 120 words). Written to sit under a photo. Starts with the moment, not the brand. Suggest the photo in one line before the caption: *Photo idea: …* Up to three hashtags, specific ones (#vermontcheese, the style, the town), never #foodie or #artisan.
2. **Facebook post** (60 to 150 words). A little more context, slightly more conversational, aimed at the local community and regulars. Can mention where to buy or a farm-store day if the maker has confirmed it.
3. **Newsletter blurb** (80 to 180 words). For subscribers who already like them. Can go one layer deeper into the story, and end with something to do: try a pairing (as a suggestion), visit, order.

Each post uses a different moment. Together they should feel like the same farm.

## Rules

- One moment per post. A moment is something that happened or happens: a time, a place, an action.
- Public-facing: only maker-confirmed or sourced facts. If a post needs a detail you don't have, leave a gap: `[ask: what time do the cows go out in the morning?]`
- No slop words, no exclamation points stacked up, no emoji strings. One emoji is fine if the maker's own voice uses them; otherwise none.
- Write in the maker's voice, using their own phrases from the interview where you can.
- Honor every preference the maker has given.

## Weak and strong

Weak:
> 🧀✨ Nothing beats a taste of Vermont! Our handcrafted cheeses are made with love and passion right here on our family farm. Stop by and taste the difference! #foodie #cheese #artisan #local

Strong (fictional Birch Hollow Creamery, Instagram):
> *Photo idea: the herd walking out through the gate, early light.*
>
> First morning on pasture. Twenty-two Jerseys, through the gate at 5:40. From today until October, every wheel of Hollow Tomme we make comes from this grass. We'll taste the difference in about six months. `[ask: what's one thing you notice in the milk the first week on pasture?]`
>
> #vermontcheese #tomme #`[town]`

## Self-check

- Does each post start with a moment, not a claim?
- Are the three moments different?
- Does every fact trace to the maker or a source?
- Could another creamery post this unchanged? If yes, it's too generic.
