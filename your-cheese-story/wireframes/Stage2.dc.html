Idea 1 · Chosen Ancestors My pick

An ancient answer to a Vermont problem

Your land is too rocky for big dairy. So the family turned to Alpine cheese, a way of making cheese that mountain people worked out thousands of years ago for the same kind of problem. You can taste that place in it. And you made it your own.

Why I lean this way: it's the one only you can tell. It ties the land, a very old tradition, and what you changed into one story.
