# Video Concept

**Purpose:** A short video a small creamery can actually make with a phone on an ordinary day: 60 to 90 seconds that carries one story.

## Who reads it and how

The maker, or whoever on the farm holds the phone. They are busy, not filmmakers, and will shoot this between chores. The finished video goes on social media, the website, or a monger's screen.

## What it needs from the Creamery Card

The chosen story, the physical places and actions on the farm (milking, the make room, the cave, the farm store), and any recurring events that could be filmed later.

## Structure

1. **The hook** (the first 3 seconds). One image or one line that makes someone stop scrolling. Describe the shot and, if there's voiceover, the first words. It should come from the story, not from a generic beauty shot.
2. **Shot list** (6 to 10 shots). Each one: what's in frame, roughly how long (in seconds), and a filming note a non-filmmaker can follow ("hold the phone still on the gate post," "film from the cheese's height"). Everything must be filmable on an ordinary working day.
3. **Voiceover script** (about 120 to 200 words for 60 to 90 seconds). Spoken by the maker in their own voice. Short sentences. Paired to the shots.
4. **On-screen text** (optional). Two or three short lines, for viewers watching without sound.
5. **Capture next time.** Two or three moments to film when they happen (the first day on pasture, a calving, washing rinds after a new batch), and why each would strengthen the story.

Total length target: 60 to 90 seconds.

## Rules

- The voiceover and on-screen text are public-facing: only maker-confirmed or sourced facts, with gaps where something's missing.
- The shot list and "capture next time" are planning material: ideas are fine.
- Favor real work over posed moments. Hands, tools, animals, weather, sound.
- One story. A video that tries to cover the herd, the history, the awards, and the farm store in 90 seconds covers none of them.
- Honor every preference the maker has given.

## Weak and strong

Weak:
> Drone shot over rolling hills. Slow-motion cheese wheel. Text: "Crafted with passion." Montage of the family smiling. Logo.

Strong (fictional Birch Hollow Creamery, opening):
> **Hook:** A hand pulls a wheel off the shelf in silence. Voiceover: "This cheese only exists half the year."
> **Shots:** 1) Close on a calendar on the make-room wall, May circled (3 s). 2) The gate opening, cows walking through, phone braced on the post (8 s). 3) Milk going into the vat, filmed from the side (6 s). …
> **Voiceover (excerpt):** "From May to October, our 22 Jerseys are on grass, and every wheel we make comes from that milk. In November we stop. `[ask: what do you do in the make room in winter?]`"
> **Capture next time:** The first morning back on pasture in spring. It's the story's turning point, and it happens once a year.

## Self-check

- Could someone on the farm shoot every shot this week?
- Is there one story, not a tour?
- Does every fact in the voiceover trace to the maker or a source?
- Is it 60 to 90 seconds when read aloud with the shots?
