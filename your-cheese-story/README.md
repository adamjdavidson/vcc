# Your Cheese Story

Planning, knowledge files and wireframes for **Your Cheese Story**, a conversation-first app that helps Vermont artisan cheesemakers find and tell the story of their creamery and cheeses. It's built for the Vermont Cheese Council workshop on October 14, 2026, and draws on the Vermont Cheese Story Kit in the rest of this repository.

This folder mirrors the `claude/` folder of the "Cheese Narrative" Claude project. Where a document says `claude/...`, look here: for example, `claude/kit/flow.md` is `kit/flow.md`.

## Start here

- **[`your-cheese-story-spec-v2.md`](your-cheese-story-spec-v2.md)** is the source of truth for the app. It covers the five stages, the doors between stages, the AI's jobs, and a map of which knowledge file feeds which stage (section 5).
- **[`kit/flow.md`](kit/flow.md)** describes the stage model: Gathering, Story shape, Audience, The essential story, Telling it. It is a loop, never done.
- **[`kit/story-arcs.md`](kit/story-arcs.md)** holds the eight story arcs and the shared story elements, the framework the whole app runs on.

## What's here

| Folder | Contents |
|---|---|
| `kit/` | Core brief, story arcs, flow, and a guide for every output: monger card, for the table, shelf talker, packaging and label, website story, photo essay, social posts, video, press release, PR research and pitches, monger answer site, and the buyer personas used in testing |
| `kit/history/` | Style histories: Alpine, cheddar, goat, bloomy rind, washed rind, blue, tomme, Gouda-style, pasta filata, sheep's milk, plus Vermont cheese 1985–2026 |
| `kit/research/`, `research/` | Buyer research, the research on what makes a story element work, and the long review on selling artisanal food through story |
| `wireframes/` | Source files for the wireframe canvas, in the Claude Design canvas format. They need the canvas runtime to display and won't render as plain web pages. |
| `archive/` | Superseded work: the v1 spec, which had a step-by-step flow, and the twelve narrative lenses that the eight arcs replaced |

Most history files draw on Paul Kindstedt's *Cheese and Culture* (Chelsea Green, 2012). The book itself isn't included here.
